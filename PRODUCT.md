# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary Clients:** High-net-worth homeowners, apartment & duplex owners, and discerning families in Dhaka (e.g., Gulshan, Banani, Dhanmondi, Bashundhara, Uttara) seeking bespoke, turnkey luxury interior transformations from concept to handover.
- **Studio Leadership & Designers:** Studio principals, interior architects, and project managers managing incoming client briefs, project portfolios, site visit schedules, invoices, and editorial publications.

## Product Purpose

Bangla Sketch (বাংলা স্কেচ) transforms spaces into warm, soul-filled architectural sanctuaries. It bridges an editorial public showcase, interactive client exploration tools (space collections, cost estimators, interactive chat consultations), and a comprehensive studio management CMS into one unified experience.

## Positioning

"Interior spaces with soul" — Bespoke turnkey architectural craftsmanship combining modern functional elegance with authentic Bengali tactile warmth, local natural materials, and flawless end-to-end execution.

## Operating Context

- **Client Discovery:** Browsing curated portfolios, saving spaces to personal moodboards/collections, running budget estimates, and booking on-site diagnostic consultations.
- **Studio Operations:** Managing multi-phase interior project pipelines, publishing case studies and design journal entries, invoicing, and handling client communications.
- **Bilingual Resonance:** English-first interface with seamless Bengali typographic accents (Noto Serif / Noto Sans Bengali) and cultural authenticity.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Client-Facing Features:**
  - Interactive portfolio gallery with category filters and rich project details.
  - Custom space collection drawer (saved moodboard items stored locally & synced).
  - Real-time cost estimator and conversational design assistant widget.
  - Site-visit booking system with automated confirmation.
  - Design journal (blog) with rich editorial typography and social sharing.
- **Studio Admin & Operations:**
  - JWT-authenticated CMS dashboard for projects, journal, testimonials, and video content.
  - Automated invoice generation, payment status tracking, and PDF export workflows.
  - Cloudinary-optimized media pipeline with AVIF/WebP delivery and upload signatures.
  - Rate limiting, security headers, and asynchronous background worker queues (SMTP & cron).

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone & Voice:** Warm, refined, poetic, authoritative, hospitable, and deeply attentive to architectural craftsmanship.
- **Visual Identity:** Warm architectural canvas (`#FAF7F2`), deep charcoal typography, muted earthy terracotta/sage accents, serif headlines (Instrument Serif & Noto Serif Bengali), clean geometric body text (Manrope & Noto Sans Bengali).
- **Core Promise:** No cookie-cutter interiors; every space is crafted as an authentic sanctuary tailored to the occupant's rhythm of life.

## Evidence on Hand

- 200+ completed residential & architectural projects across Dhaka spanning 10+ years of practice.
- Documented project photography and editorial case studies in Dhaka luxury neighborhoods.
- Real client testimonials, studio team profiles, and operational invoice records.

## Product Principles

1. **Soul Over Spectacle:** Prioritize warmth, living comfort, and spatial harmony over hollow, trendy ostentation.
2. **Turnkey Integrity:** Every line drawn on paper must translate faithfully into physical craftsmanship on site.
3. **Frictionless Discovery:** Give prospective clients immediate clarity on aesthetic possibilities, spatial costs, and design collaboration steps.
4. **Bespoke Respect:** Treat each client's home as a personal narrative rather than a generic template.
5. **Craft in Every Touchpoint:** From physical millwork to digital interactions, every detail reflects precision and care.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA color contrast across light warm themes and dark administrative surfaces.
- Fully responsive mobile-first layouts designed for fluid touch interaction across smartphone and desktop viewports.
- Clear semantic HTML, keyboard-navigable interactive modals/drawers, and explicit ARIA labels for assistive technologies.
